Quick answers

How the directory works

What is RoofCoil?

RoofCoil is a free directory of metal roofing suppliers and fabricators. Contractors search it by ability — what a shop can actually run, from snap-lock and mechanical seam standing seam panels to slit coil, custom trim and 3D parts — by the color a job is spec'd in, or by location, and see each shop's full capability list before calling.

How do I find a metal roofing supplier or fabricator near me?

Open Find a supplier, pick what the job needs — a panel profile, coil and flats, trim, or on-site roll forming — and allow location access. Shops sort closest-first with the mileage shown on each listing, and you can narrow to a 25, 50, 100 or 250 mile radius or type a city instead.

How do I get my company listed?

Fill out the Get Listed application with your abilities, gauges, colors, plant locations and color chart. A person reviews every application and gets in contact before anything goes live — once published, contractors find you by ability, color and location.

What can I search by?

Thirty-six abilities, including a full panel breakdown — 1″, 1.5″ and 2″ mechanical lock, 1.5″/1.75″ snap-lock, nail strip, T-panel (138T/238T), batten seam, 2.5″ and 3″ trapezoidal, exposed fastener, flush wall & soffit, board & batten — roll-formed gutters (5″–7″ K-style, 5″ and 6″ half-round, 6″–8″ box, 6″ euro box, fascia and straight-face) in five materials from .027 aluminum to 20 oz copper — plus painted and bare coil, trim & flashing, 3D parts and on-site roll forming. Then narrow by the stock colors each shop carries and by location, with shops sorted by GPS distance.

Can I price a job here too?

Yes — free member tools include a standing seam panel calculator, a trim profile drawing app that turns your sketch into a cut list, and a Color Lab that renders any stock color on a live panel.
