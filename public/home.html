Quick answers

How RoofCoil works

What is RoofCoil?

RoofCoil is the search engine for everything sheet metal in North America. It indexes suppliers, fabricators and contractors by what they can actually run — snap-lock and mechanical seam standing seam panels, slit coil and flat sheet, custom trim, roll-formed gutters, 3D parts and on-site roll forming — plus the colors they carry and where they are, so you can locate the material or the shop for a job in a couple of taps. It also has free specialized tools: a panel calculator, a trim drawing app and a Color Lab.

How do I find a metal roofing supplier or fabricator near me?

Open Find a supplier, pick what the job needs — a panel profile, coil and flats, trim, or on-site roll forming — and allow location access. Shops sort closest-first with the mileage shown on each listing, and you can narrow to a 25, 50, 100 or 250 mile radius or type a city instead.

How do I get my company listed?

Fill out the Get Listed application with your abilities, gauges, colors, plant locations and color chart. A person reviews every application and gets in contact before anything goes live — once published, contractors find you by ability, color and location.

What can I search by?

Forty-three abilities, including a full panel breakdown — 1″, 1.5″ (single or double) and 2″ mechanical lock, 1.5″ and 1.75″ snap-lock, 1″ and 1.5″ nail strip, 138T and 238T T-panel, batten seam, 2.5″ and 3″ trapezoidal, exposed fastener, flush wall & soffit, board & batten — roll-formed gutters (5″–7″ K-style, 5″ and 6″ half-round, 6″–8″ box, 6″ euro box, fascia and straight-face) in .027/.032 aluminum, 26/24 ga painted steel, Galvalume, paint-grip and copper, with on-site panel and gutter roll forming — plus painted and bare coil, trim & flashing, 3D parts and on-site roll forming. Then narrow by the stock colors each shop carries and by location, with shops sorted by GPS distance.

Can I price a job here too?

Yes — free member tools include a standing seam panel calculator, a trim profile drawing app that turns your sketch into a cut list, and a Color Lab that renders any stock color on a live panel.
