Terms for shops

Plans, billing, how orders are routed, and who sees a shop's data. A draft for review.

1Plans

Every shop is listed in the directory free. A plan adds ordering through RoofCoil. Prices are shown on your plan card and at checkout, before tax.

Featured is an add-on on any plan, Listing included (section 5). “The first” location is the first one with a map pin, else the listing's own pin.

To take orders, a shop needs all four: a plan that takes orders (Shop or Pro, or a pilot or comp from RoofCoil), an order email, its own saved prices, and its ordering switch on. Missing any one, the shop keeps its listing and is simply not offered for orders, and Shop Settings says what is missing.

When a shop has more people than its plan covers, nothing is deleted: owners and the longest-standing staff members keep access, and the newest staff members are inactive until the plan covers them again.

Pilots and comps. RoofCoil may cover a shop's plan, or Featured, for a time. Each pilot or comp is noted, records who granted it, and shows on the shop's plan card with its end date; a Featured comp always has one.

2Trials

  • Shop and Pro start with a 30-day trial, no card needed to start, once per shop.
  • Add a card before the trial ends to keep the plan. A trial that ends with no card on file ends the plan: the shop is back on Listing, with its switch, prices and customers untouched.
  • A pilot from RoofCoil is the shop's trial. Start a plan before the pilot ends and the plan's trial runs to the pilot's end date, so the first charge falls on that date and no day is paid for twice. Start after the pilot has ended and the plan is charged at once.
  • A comp from RoofCoil does not use up the trial.

3Billing, failed payments and cancellation

Plans are billed monthly through Stripe, which holds your card; RoofCoil never sees the card number. Receipts, invoices and payment emails come from Stripe and go to the shop's owner.

If a payment fails, ordering continues for 14 days while Stripe retries the card, and you are emailed the cut-off date. From day 15, ordering is paused while Stripe keeps retrying. If the retries run out, Stripe cancels the plan and the shop is back on Listing. Update your card in Shop Floor → Shop Settings, and a paused shop takes orders again as soon as the payment goes through.

Cancel any time in the billing portal (Shop Floor → Shop Settings → Manage billing). The plan runs to the end of the period already paid for, and you can renew it in the portal until then; the shop keeps what it paid for, and there is no partial refund. When the plan ends, ordering through RoofCoil pauses. Your listing, prices, customers and orders are kept. Everyone on the shop keeps access for 30 days after the plan stops; after that Listing covers 2 people, and the newest staff members beyond that are inactive. Nothing is deleted.

4Upgrades and downgrades

Switch between Shop and Pro in the billing portal. The change is immediate both ways, and prorated: an upgrade is invoiced for the rest of the period when you make it, and a downgrade credits the unused time to your next invoice. A downgrade's limits apply at once: people beyond the new plan's count (the newest staff members first) become inactive until it covers them again, and only the first location is used to suggest the shop.

5Featured

Featured is paid placement. A Featured shop is pinned first in the directory and in the app's shop list, always with a “Featured” label. It is sold on any plan, Listing included, runs as its own subscription and is cancelled on its own, at the end of its period. RoofCoil may also comp Featured for a dated period, shown the same way.

Featured never decides where an order goes. It plays no part in which shop RoofCoil suggests or preselects for a contractor.

6How orders are routed

RoofCoil sends your order to the shop you choose, or suggests the nearest shop taking orders to your ZIP. RoofCoil is run by the owner of Fortified Sheet Metal, one of these shops; it gets no preference. Sample prices are RoofCoil's illustrative numbers, not any shop's.

The same words are printed under the shop picker in the Panel & Trim app. “Nearest” is the straight-line distance from the contractor's ZIP to the shop locations its plan covers; Featured and shop names play no part. An order goes only to the shop shown under “Send this order to” when the contractor sends it. With no shop chosen or suggested, the app asks the contractor to choose one.

7Who can see your shop's data

  • Fortified Sheet Metal's team sees nothing of another shop beyond what any signed-in contractor sees: its sell prices, never its costs, orders, customers or settings.
  • RoofCoil's platform admins use logins separate from any shop's people. They read a shop's private data (its orders, customer list and tiers, costs and private settings) only under a read-only support grant that the shop's owner opens from Shop Settings. A grant lasts 24 hours and never more than 7 days, the owner can revoke it at any time and sees every active and past grant, and it never lets anyone change anything. RoofCoil runs the accounts themselves (sign-in support), so platform admins do see account details and who runs which shop.
  • No admin path changes another shop's order email, ordering switch, link name, PO numbers, roster or orders. Platform admins can suspend a shop as moderation (which stops its ordering), see its plan and billing status, and comp a plan or Featured; each comp records the admin and shows on the shop's plan card. Invoices and cards stay in Stripe.
  • An owned listing changes owner only by its owner's own invite, or after 7 days' emailed notice to its current owners, who can refuse it.
  • Contractors' ZIPs are never shown to shops. A shop sees a contractor's name and phone once the contractor has ordered from it, or if the contractor was already its customer before plans began. An account a shop adds by email shows only the email the shop typed until the contractor orders.

The honest limit. RoofCoil is run by the owner of Fortified Sheet Metal. Separate logins separate these permissions, make any access to another shop visible and require that shop's consent, and the admin login cannot repoint another shop's orders. They cannot stop the operator from using the database owner role or the service key, which bypass these rules. That is why the rules, and this limit, are published here.

8Orders

No money moves through RoofCoil on orders. Contractors pay the shop directly, on the shop's own terms, and RoofCoil takes no fee on an order. An order carries an estimate from the shop's own saved prices, and the shop confirms the price with the contractor. RoofCoil emails each order to the shop's order email, and texts the shop's alert number when one is set.

9Contact

Questions about these terms: orders@roofcoil.com or 972-944-7963. How RoofCoil handles personal data is in the privacy policy.

Once final, these are the terms a shop accepts at checkout when it starts a plan. Until then this draft is not in effect.
