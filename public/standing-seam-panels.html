RoofCoil directory

Standing seam metal roof panels, rolled to length

Snap-lock · mechanical seam · structural · flush wall & soffit — shop-rolled or run on your job site

Fabricators listed on RoofCoil roll-form standing seam metal roofing panels run to the exact length your roof needs — no factory lead times, no freight damage — in the shop, or on site with portable machines when the run is too long to truck. Pick the profile, the gauge, the finish and the color, and the panel calculator prices the job in seconds.

Profiles you'll find

1" & 1.5" mechanical seam

SS100 (1") and SS150 (1.5") double-lock mechanical seam panels — the low-slope workhorses, field-seamed for a weather-tight roof down to shallow pitches.

1.5" snap-lock

SS450 snap-lock — the most common residential standing seam profile. No seaming tool, clip-fastened, clean straight ribs with optional striations or pencil ribs.

2" mechanical seam

SSQ200 2" mechanical seam for low-slope and commercial roofs, with ARMCO-style options for high-wind markets.

1.75" structural snap-lock

SSQ675 — a heavier 22–24 gauge structural snap-lock that spans open purlins on barns, shops and metal buildings.

Fastener flange & flush wall

FF100 nail-strip panel (no clips) and FWQ100 flush wall & soffit panel for walls, soffits and porch ceilings.

Trapezoidal & Ultra Seam

Symmetrical trapezoidal profiles for commercial work, plus the Adax Metals Ultra Seam line (US-100, US-150, US-175, US-200) from Weatherford.

Gauges, finishes and colors

Panels run in 24 and 26 gauge painted steel (22 gauge on structural profiles) with PVDF (Kynar 500®) or SMP paint systems, plus bare Galvalume, G90 galvanized, bonderized and copper. Stock colors include Regal White, Charcoal, Medium and Dark Bronze, Matte Black, Evergreen, Copper Metallic, Galvalume and more — try any of them on a live panel in the Color Lab.

Shop-rolled or rolled on your job site

Shorter runs are rolled in the shop for pickup or delivery, and long panels — anything you would rather not truck — are run on site with a portable roll former. Pricing varies by shop; as an example, Fortified Sheet Metal includes the first 40 miles from its nearest base, then $2 per mile one way, with a $200 shop minimum and a $600 job-site minimum. Coil up to 24" wide is priced online; wider coil is quoted by the shop.
