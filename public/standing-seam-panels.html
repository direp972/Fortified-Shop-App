Standing seam profiles you'll find

1" & 1.5" mechanical seam

SS100 (1") and SS150 (1.5") double-lock mechanical seam panels — the low-slope workhorses, field-seamed for a weather-tight roof down to shallow pitches.

1.5" snap-lock

SS450 snap-lock — the most common residential standing seam profile. No seaming tool, clip-fastened, clean straight ribs with optional striations or pencil ribs.

2" mechanical seam

SSQ200 2" mechanical seam for low-slope and commercial roofs, with ARMCO-style options for high-wind markets.

1.75" structural snap-lock

SSQ675 — a heavier 22–24 gauge structural snap-lock that spans open purlins on barns, shops and metal buildings.

Fastener flange & flush wall

FF100 nail-strip panel (no clips) and FWQ100 flush wall & soffit panel for walls, soffits and porch ceilings.

Trapezoidal & Ultra Seam

Symmetrical trapezoidal profiles for commercial work, plus the Adax Metals Ultra Seam line (US-100, US-150, US-175, US-200) from Weatherford.

Exposed fastener profiles you'll find

Screw-down panels go by a different trade name at every mill, so listed shops mark the generic shape they roll. Every profile below is made by more than one manufacturer — search by the shape and you'll find whoever runs it near the job.

R-panel / PBR

The general-purpose screw-down workhorse — 1-1/4" ribs on 12" centers, 36" coverage, roof and wall. PBR adds a purlin-bearing leg at the lap.

7.2 panel (structural rib)

Deep symmetric 1-1/2" trapezoids every 7.2" — the structural and industrial pick, usually 22–24 gauge over open purlins.

7/8″ corrugated

The classic deep wave on a 2-2/3" pitch. Coverage depends on the lap — two corrugations for roofing, one for siding — so quote the application.

1/2″ corrugated (low-profile)

Same 2-2/3" pitch, shallower wave — walls, wainscot and equipment screens, with roofing use on some lines.

5V-crimp

Paired V-crimps on a flat pan, 24" coverage — the porch-and-farmhouse classic, in 26 gauge Galvalume or 29 gauge bare.

Ag panel / tuff rib

3/4" trapezoid ribs with minor flutes between — the 29 and 26 gauge barn-and-shop panel, 36" coverage.

U-panel / PBU

Squared 3/4" U-ribs every 6" — liner panel and light-duty screw-down for walls and roofs; PBU adds the purlin-bearing leg.

M-panel / box-rib (wall)

Squared 3/4" ribs on a wide pan, 36" or 42" coverage — wall and equipment-screen duty rather than structural roof.

Gauges, finishes and colors

Standing seam runs in 24 and 26 gauge painted steel (22 gauge on structural profiles) and exposed fastener in 26 and 29 gauge (22–24 on 7.2 and heavier duty) with PVDF (Kynar 500®) or SMP paint systems, plus bare Galvalume, G90 galvanized, bonderized and copper. Stock colors include Regal White, Charcoal, Medium and Dark Bronze, Matte Black, Evergreen, Copper Metallic, Galvalume and more — try any of them on a live panel in the Color Lab.

Shop-rolled or rolled on your job site

Shorter runs are rolled in the shop for pickup or delivery, and long panels — anything you would rather not truck — are run on site with a portable roll former. Pricing varies by shop; as an example, Fortified Sheet Metal includes the first 40 miles from its nearest base, then $2 per mile one way, with a $200 shop minimum and a $600 job-site minimum. Coil up to 24" wide is priced online; wider coil is quoted by the shop.
