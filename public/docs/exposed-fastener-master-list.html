How to read this list. Each family is the generic shape a contractor would name; the rows underneath are every individual product the five manufacturers roll in that shape, with each maker's trade name linked to its product page. Specs come from the manufacturers' own spec sheets and product pages (verified Aug 19, 2026) — confirm the exact drawing before ordering.

Quality Metals here is the Texas company (San Antonio · Dallas · Houston · McAllen). Two Quality Metals wall systems were excluded as concealed-fastener; all standing seam, nail-strip/fastener-flange, flush, and board-and-batten systems from every maker are likewise out of scope. A former PAC-CLAD R-41 and Drexel R-41 are discontinued and omitted.
