Metal coil & flat sheet supply

Painted and bare stock for roofing contractors and sheet metal shops

Run your own machine or bend your own trim? Suppliers in the RoofCoil directory carry the raw metal: slit coil in the widths standing seam and trim shops actually use, and 4' × 10' flat sheet for brakes and shears. Painted steel in PVDF (Kynar 500®) and SMP systems from Fortified Metal, Una-Clad, Adax Metals and Berridge, plus bare Galvalume, G90 galvanized, bonderized and copper — picked up at the shop, or delivered.

What suppliers stock

Slit coil

Coil slit to width for panel machines and trim — priced per linear foot in 16n, 21n and 24n width brackets. Wider coil quoted by the shop.

Flat sheet

4' × 10' flat sheet, priced per sheet with cutting and processing included, for trim, flashing and fabricated parts.

Painted steel

24 and 26 gauge PVDF and SMP in a full architectural color range — whites, grays, bronzes, black, greens, reds and metallics.

Bare & specialty

Galvalume, G90 galvanized (16–24 ga), bonderized / paint-grip, and bare copper in 16 and 20 oz.

Order it like a panel

Coil and flats go through the same calculator as everything else: choose the brand, gauge, finish and color, enter the coil width and footage or the sheet count, and you get a per-foot and per-sheet price with the total — the same shop that rolls panels fills the coil order, so the color you order for trim stock is the exact color your panels were run from.
